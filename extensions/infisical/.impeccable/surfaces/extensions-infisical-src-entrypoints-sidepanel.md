## Direction contract

THESIS: A secure ledger for secrets: warm paper and ink, hairline rules, and a visible environment rail. Refuse the generic dashboard card grid and the neon-on-black terminal costume.

OWN-WORLD: Bone paper or deep ink-green ground; bottle-green selection; vermilion only for destructive risk; Schibsted Grotesk for UI; Geist Mono for keys, values, and counts; 1px rules, tabular numerals, stamped environment labels.

STORY: The user sees the scope first, scans a compact index of secret keys, then deliberately reveals, copies, or edits one entry. Every dangerous action names the project, environment, and path.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow environment rail on the left, project/environment scope band on top, search and path controls, then a dense secret index with masked values. The primary “New secret” action sits in the scope band; the selected entry opens the leaf inspector on the right at wide widths.

FORM: The Secure Ledger, brief-pinned in the approved SPEC; seed: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
